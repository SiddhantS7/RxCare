Medicine Name: Azithromycin

Category: Antibiotic (Macrolide)

Uses:
- Respiratory infections
- Skin infections

Standard Dosage:
- Adults: 500 mg once daily for 3 days
- Children: Weight-based dosing

Age Suitability:
- Adults and children

Contraindications:
- Liver disease

Warnings:
- May cause QT prolongation
