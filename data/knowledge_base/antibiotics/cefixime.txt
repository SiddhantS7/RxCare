Medicine Name: Cefixime

Category: Antibiotic (Cephalosporin)

Uses:
- Respiratory infections
- Urinary tract infections

Standard Dosage:
- Adults: 200–400 mg daily
- Children: Weight-based

Age Suitability:
- Adults and children

Contraindications:
- Cephalosporin allergy

Warnings:
- Complete prescribed course
