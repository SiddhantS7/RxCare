Medicine Name: Ciprofloxacin

Category: Antibiotic (Fluoroquinolone)

Uses:
- Urinary tract infections
- Gastrointestinal infections

Standard Dosage:
- Adults: 250–500 mg every 12 hours

Age Suitability:
- Adults only

Contraindications:
- Children
- Tendon disorders

Warnings:
- Risk of tendon rupture
