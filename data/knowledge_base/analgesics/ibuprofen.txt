Medicine Name: Ibuprofen

Category: NSAID (Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory)

Uses:
- Pain
- Inflammation
- Fever

Standard Dosage:
- Adults: 200–400 mg every 6–8 hours
- Children: Dose based on weight

Age Suitability:
- Adults and children over 6 months
- Avoid in infants unless prescribed

Contraindications:
- Peptic ulcer disease
- Kidney impairment

Warnings:
- Prolonged use may cause gastrointestinal bleeding
- Take with food to reduce stomach irritation
