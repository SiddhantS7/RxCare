Medicine Name: Aspirin

Category: NSAID, Antiplatelet

Uses:
- Pain
- Fever
- Prevention of blood clots (low dose)

Standard Dosage:
- Adults: 300–600 mg every 4–6 hours
- Children: Not recommended

Age Suitability:
- Adults only

Contraindications:
- Children (Reye’s syndrome risk)
- Bleeding disorders

Warnings:
- Risk of stomach bleeding
