Medicine Name: Diclofenac

Category: NSAID

Uses:
- Musculoskeletal pain
- Arthritis
- Post-operative pain

Standard Dosage:
- Adults: 50 mg up to 3 times daily
- Children: Not routinely recommended

Age Suitability:
- Adults only unless prescribed

Contraindications:
- Heart disease
- Peptic ulcer disease

Warnings:
- Increased cardiovascular risk with prolonged use
